# Community code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, avoid personal attacks,
harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing and unwanted contact. Keep technical
conversation relevant, welcome newcomers and accept corrections.

This applies to issues, PRs and discussions in this repository. The maintainer
may edit/remove disruptive content, lock threads or restrict participation,
proportionately to the behavior and its repetition.

Report abuse privately through GitHub's reporting tools. Do not expose private
information in public reports. The maintainer is subject to the same standards.
